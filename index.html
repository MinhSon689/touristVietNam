Famous tourist destinations in Vietnam

1. Ha Long Bay

  • Located in Northeastern Vietnam.
  • Ha Long Bay is the most famous tourist destination in Vietnam.
  • It is recognized by UNESCO as a World Heritage Site.

2. Hoi An Old Town

  • Located in Hoi An, Vietnam.
  • Hoi An Old Town is a heritage site and ancient trading post.
  • This old town is currently listed as an UNESCO World Heritage Site.

3. Phong Nha-Ke Bang National Park

  • Located in the North Central Coast region of Vietnam.
  • Phong Nha-Ke Bang is noted for its cave and grotto systems as it is composed of 300 caves and grottos.
  • Recognized by UNESCO as a World Natural Heritage Site.

4. Hue Imperial City

  • Located in central Vietnam.
  • Hue Imperial City is a historic complex in central Vietnam. It was the political, cultural, and religious center of the Nguyen Dynasty from 1802 to 1945.
  • Recognized by UNESCO as a World Natural Heritage Site.

5. Phu Quoc Island

  • Located in the Gulf of Thailand, in the southwest of Vietnam.
  • Phu Quoc Island is considered Vietnam’s most beautiful island destination.
